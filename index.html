Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains how Luck's Printing Services ("we", "us") handles information when you message our Facebook Page and chat with our AI-powered support assistant.

1. Information we collect

When you message our Page, we receive:

  • Your Facebook name and Page-scoped user ID
  • The content of your messages, including details you share about your printing request (such as product type, size, quantity, and design details)
  • Any files or images you send us for your order

We do not collect your Facebook password, payment card details, or private profile information.

2. How we use your information

  • To answer questions about our printing services and prices
  • To prepare quotes and process orders
  • To remember your earlier messages so the conversation can continue smoothly
  • To improve our service and support quality

3. AI processing and service providers

Our chat assistant uses artificial intelligence. Your messages are processed by:

  • Meta (Facebook Messenger), which delivers messages to us
  • Open AI, which generates the assistant's replies
  • n8n, the automation software we run on our own server, hosted with Contabo
  • A PostgreSQL database on that server, which stores conversation history

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with the providers above to operate the service, or if the law requires it.

4. Data retention

We keep conversation data for up to 30 days, or as long as needed to complete your order, and then delete it.

5. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, including keeping it on a secured, access-controlled server. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete your data. To request deletion:

  1. Send a message to our Facebook Page with the words "Delete my data", or
  2. Email us at floressimon243@gmail.com with your Facebook name

We will delete your data within 5 days and confirm when it is done.

7. Children's privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
